# Source and conclusion evaluation

## Source reliability

| Grade | Meaning | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| A | Primary source, original data, official publication, or traceable first-hand evidence | Main evidence, after checking scope and date |
| B | Authoritative secondary source or review that points to primary evidence | Working evidence; verify critical numbers and quotes |
| C | General secondary content, AI summary, or internal practice with incomplete traceability | Direction or hypothesis only |
| D | Missing, inaccessible, or unattributed source | Do not present as verified fact |

## Conclusion confidence

| Grade | Meaning | Suggested wording |
|---|---|---|
| A | Directly supported; key counterevidence addressed | Confirmed within the stated boundary |
| B | Core direction supported; some extrapolation or open validation remains | Currently supported if… |
| C | Plausible explanation with insufficient evidence | Hypothesis to validate |
| D | Conflicts with evidence or lacks a critical premise | Not supported / redesign required |
